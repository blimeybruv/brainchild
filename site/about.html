About Brainchild

A free home for AI-assisted games, mods and apps that deserve another go.

What it is

Lots of good projects stop halfway: the maker runs out of time, or moves on. Brainchild is a directory where you can list one, say what state it's in, and hand it to someone who wants to carry on.

Everything here is free to play and use. Each listing points to a public GitHub repository, and many games play right in the browser.

Handing off a project

  • List it. Sign in with GitHub and choose one of your public repositories. Brainchild checks that you own it and reads its license.
  • Fill in the handoff packet: what works, what's broken, what you were going for, your CLAUDE.md or AGENTS.md, and how the art and code were made. It lets the next person, and their AI tools, start where you stopped.
  • Choose a status: up for adoption, open for collaboration, needs testers, active or archived. You can change it later on the Edit page.

Adopting a project

  • Fork the repository on GitHub, then press Adopt on the project's page and pick your fork. Brainchild checks that it really is a fork of the original.
  • You become the maintainer. The people before you stay on the project's lineage: credit is never removed.
  • Each handoff and adoption records the license at that exact commit. See the Licensing guide for what that means.

House rules

  • Free to play and use, always. No paywalls, no loot boxes.
  • An open license for the code, and for the art and sound, before a project can be put up for adoption.
  • Mods and plugins say what they're made for (a game, a browser, an editor) and ship as source with install notes.
  • Rate your project honestly and add content notes:
    • Everyone: no violence beyond cartoon or slapstick, no blood, no swearing.
    • Teen: combat and non-graphic violence, mild blood, horror themes and jump scares, moderate language, drug or alcohol references, war strategy.
    • Mature: realistic or graphic violence and gore, strong language throughout, drug use shown, disturbing themes. Mature projects are hidden unless a visitor chooses to see them, and open behind a warning.
  • Sexually explicit content isn't allowed at any rating, including mods.
  • Be kind in discussions. No harassment or hate.
