Contact and reporting

How to report a problem, what happens next, and how to reach the person who runs the site.

Reporting something

If a project, a hosted game or a comment breaks Brainchild's house rules or the law, please tell us.

  • Signed in with GitHub: use the Report button on a comment, or Report this project at the foot of a project page. You can follow what happens to your report under your account.
  • No account: use the report form. You can leave a reply-to address if you want to hear the outcome. It is kept private and deleted when your report is closed.

Say what the problem is and where it is. If you believe something is illegal, say which law or right is involved and why.

What happens to a report

  • Every report is read by the site's operator, a person. No automated tool decides.
  • If something is removed, the person who posted it is told why, in writing, and can ask once for the decision to be looked at again.
  • Removing a project hides its page. Its lineage and license records are kept.
  • Sexually explicit content is not allowed at any rating and is removed.

Other questions

For a question about one project, ask in the Discussion section on its page: its maintainer and other members will see it. For anything about the site itself, use the report form and choose "Something else".

Brainchild is run by an individual operator. We don't publish an email address; the routes above reach the same person.
